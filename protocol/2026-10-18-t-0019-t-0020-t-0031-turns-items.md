## What changed

- **T-0031, core** (`internal/game/party.go`, `game.go`, `internal/script/timer.go`, `script.go`):
  - Every hook's `player` has `player.id` (`p1`, `p2`, ...). Players of one world get different names: `traveler`, `traveler 2`, ...
  - `room.players()`: the players in the caller's room, in the order they came in.
  - `on_leave(player)` runs when a player leaves the world. It can write `state.world`; its output is dropped; it cannot ask or move.
  - Room timers: `timer.start/cancel/left` with `{room = true}`, at most 10 per room. Everyone in the room sees a shown one; players see it appear and go as they move. When it runs out, `on_timer(player, name, "room")` runs once, for the starter or the player there longest. In an empty room it is dropped.
  - Client: up to 20 countdowns (10 of the player, 10 of the room); room timers have the id `room:<name>`.
- **T-0019, Lua** (`content/demo/lib/round.lua`): `round.turns/free/done/next/current/may_act/leave/status/changed/timeout`, hooks `on_turn`, `on_round_end`, `on_turn_timeout`. The round lives in `state.world`; the turn's deadline is a room timer.
- **T-0020, Lua** (`content/demo/lib/items.lua`): take rules `each`, `once`, `count`, `split` on `state.world.update`; `items.visible`, `items.release`; effects that wear off after seconds or moves (`items.bless`, `items.move`, `on_effect_end`).
- **Demo:** the guard room's back door now leads to a forest clearing (`scripts/clearing.lua`), where escaped players meet: a dice game in turns, a purse split among the players there, apples (each), a horn (once, back by the fire when its owner leaves), three torches (count) and a shrine's blessing (a minute or five moves). The path home ends the story.
- **Docs:** `docs/lua-sandbox.md`, `docs/pack-patterns.md` (rounds, shared items), `docs/websocket-protocol.md`, `docs/testing.md`.
- **Tasks:** GM commands for turns moved to T-0011, a restart keeping the round and never-repeating player ids to T-0018.

## Reviews

**test-engineer:**
- Added `internal/script/party_test.go`, `internal/game/party_edge_test.go`, `libs_edge_test.go`, `libs_bugs_test.go` and the fuzz target `FuzzPartyOps` (`party_fuzz_test.go`).
- Found 3 bugs in the Lua libraries, all fixed:
  - `items.take` lost the item when the player's bag could not be written. Now the bag is filled inside the world update, and nothing is taken when it fails.
  - `round.status` kept saying "X took too long" for the rest of the round. Now only until the next turn ends.
  - An effect with only seconds never wore off when its timer ran out in a room without the library. Now `items.blessed` ends it.
- Also fixed from its notes: a player who was there when the purse was split but got no coin now hears "not enough coins for everyone" instead of "shared before you came".

**security-reviewer:**
- Medium: a player out of Lua time who started a room timer kept it from ever running (holding up a round). Fixed: the next player in the room runs it; only when all are out of time does it wait. The countdown's removal is sent once.
- Medium: room timer changes were pushed to every player in the room after every hook, so one client could flood the others. Fixed: deliveries at most every 250 ms per world, the latest state per timer.
- Low: `on_leave` did not run when the leaver was out of Lua time, so the horn stayed gone. Fixed: it runs anyway and is charged.
- Low: a closed session's queue stayed in memory while a room timer it started ran. Fixed: `Close` clears it.
- Low: the `count` rule's comment said "one each". Fixed: "first come, first served".
- Low, documented: names are reused, so packs must keep `player.id`, not names, to know who did what (the libraries do).
- Low, moved to T-0018: ids restart at `p1` with the world. Harmless while `state.world` lasts only as long as the world runs.
- Checked and fine: the read-only rule of `on_change` cannot be bypassed through room timers or `on_leave`, locking, the e2e origin guard (only adds the local port 18767), no new external URLs, no secrets.

## Findings

- A split counts every connection in the room, so extra tabs lower the others' shares. That is the demo's rule; a pack can choose otherwise.
- `on_turn` usually runs from `on_change`, so it may only say things and set items; questions and world writes belong to the player's own clicks. Documented in `docs/pack-patterns.md`.
