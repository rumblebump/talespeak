## What changed

- **Store** (migration `0003_world_lobby.sql`): worlds get a title, a visibility (public or private), their creator and a `home` flag for the main world of a pack. `CreateWorld` counts the account's and the server's worlds in the same transaction and makes the creator GM. `ListWorlds` lists the account's own worlds first, then public ones, the newest first, at most 200. Invites name their world; a world keeps at most 100 that still work.
- **Hub** (`internal/hub`, new): the `ws.Game` of the server. It loads a world on its first player, unloads it after `TALESPEAK_WORLD_IDLE` (5 min), and caps loaded worlds (`TALESPEAK_LOADED_WORLDS`, 20; the world empty longest unloads for a new one). The main world stays loaded and counts against no cap. One address (IPv6: its /48) plays at most `TALESPEAK_WORLDS_PER_ADDRESS` (4) other worlds at once. Ending a world sends its players back to the lobby before its state is deleted.
- **Accounts:** `GET /api/worlds` (the lobby), `POST /api/worlds` (create), `POST /api/finish` (end), `world` on `/api/invites` and `/ws?world=ID`, `"joined"` on `/api/join`. Settings `TALESPEAK_CREATE_WORLDS` (everyone or gm), `TALESPEAK_PACKS`, `TALESPEAK_WORLDS_PER_ACCOUNT` (3), `TALESPEAK_MAX_WORLDS` (1000). `talespeak invite -world ID`.
- **Packs:** optional `description` in pack.yaml (at most 300 bytes). The catalog is `TALESPEAK_PACK`, every folder in `TALESPEAK_PACKS` and the built-in demo.
- **Web client:** the lobby with a card per world (name, pack and its description, public or private, creator, players, online now, since when, your role; play, join code, end world), a join-code box, a new-world form; a **worlds** button goes back; `#world=N` keeps a reload in its world.
- Docs: accounts.md, admin-limits.md, websocket-protocol.md, web-client.md, content-format.md, setup-server.md; deploy/compose.yml and staging.env (`TALESPEAK_CREATE_WORLDS=everyone`).

## Reviews

**security-reviewer:** authorization per world id held everywhere (WebSocket, invites, finish), no edu bypass, no SQL built from strings, no HTML sinks in the client. Findings:
- High: one network could hold every loaded-world slot, the main world included. Fixed: the main world stays loaded and outside the cap; one address plays at most 4 other worlds at once (`TALESPEAK_WORLDS_PER_ADDRESS`).
- Medium: the per-address Lua time and memory budgets count per world, so they grow with the worlds one address plays. Narrowed by the per-address world limit to 5× at most; a budget shared by all worlds is noted in T-0010.
- Medium: newer public worlds could push an account's own worlds out of the 200 listed, and one IPv6 network could create many worlds. Fixed: own worlds first; creating worlds and registering count IPv6 by /48 (`clientip.Wide`). Paging or search for admins is noted in T-0011.
- Medium (kids safety): world names are free text every signed-in account sees. Narrowed: blank-looking letters and more than 2 combining marks on a letter are removed; a word filter is T-0006, noted there; kids servers should run in edu mode, where worlds are listed only to members.
- Low: no limit on join codes. Fixed: 20 at once and one a minute per account, 100 live codes per world (409).
- Low: a join racing the end of a world could write a character into it. Fixed: a closed world with a store refuses players before loading them.
- Low: the hub waited for worlds' locks under its own. Fixed: it snapshots, then asks the worlds without its lock.
- Low: an upgrade opened servers kept closed with `TALESPEAK_WORLD_ACCESS=invite`. Fixed: creating defaults to `gm` there.
- Low: 404 against 403 told whether a private world existed. Fixed: private worlds the account may not see answer 404 everywhere.
- Low: long names overflowed the card. Fixed: they wrap.
- Low: a former main world could never be ended. Fixed: only the current main world is protected; admins end former ones.

**test-engineer:** about 60 edge tests in `internal/hub/hub_edge_test.go`, `internal/game/lifecycle_edge_test.go`, `internal/account/worlds_edge_test.go` (`FuzzCleanTitle`, `FuzzWorldParam`), `internal/store/sqlite/worlds_edge_test.go`, `internal/pack/description_test.go`, `cmd/talespeak/packs_test.go`: concurrent loads, finish and close while loading, reaping and eviction racing joins (never a dead session), the per-address limit under 40 joins at once, a real hub behind the API with 8 concurrent ends. Findings:
- A world loaded for its first player could be evicted before they entered it. Fixed: worlds someone is joining are not evicted.
- `world=+5` and `world=005` were accepted. Fixed: ids only in the form the lobby writes.
- The zero-width joiner is removed, so emoji sequences split. Kept for now (it is also a way to hide text); noted in T-0006.
- A refused create still uses a rate-limit token. Kept: the limit protects the write either way.
