## What changed

- `internal/script`:
  - `say`, `ui.dialog`, `ui.select` and `ui.ask` take `from`: who says the line or asks, 1 to 40 bytes on one line.
  - It travels in `Voice.From`.
- `internal/ws`, `internal/game`: `text`, `dialog`, `select` and `ask` messages carry `from`.
- Web client:
  - Shows the name before the line or the question, with a mark that sets the line apart from the story.
  - Labels the question group "Whisp: ...".
  - Reads the line with a second local voice of the page's language, or the same voice a little higher when there is only one.
- Demo pack:
  - The block of helpers that each room copied is now `content/demo/lib/rooms.lua`, and every room calls `require("rooms")`.
  - `whisp` and `whisp_ask` use `from = "Whisp"`.
- Test pack: typing "guide" in the hall shows a guide line and a guide question.
- Docs: `docs/lua-sandbox.md`, `docs/websocket-protocol.md`, `docs/pack-patterns.md`.
